Which sums of 1/(2ⁿ − 1) are irrational?

Erdős asked whether ∑a∈A​1/(2a−1) is irrational for every infinite set A of positive integers. The question is open. The papers here prove that the sum is irrational when A satisfies a weighted summability condition, when A has a summable divisor cover, and when A lies in the union of a set of each kind. Each result holds with any integer b≥2 in place of 2. The simplest case is the one Erdős stated in 1968: every infinite A with ∑a∈A​1/a<∞.
